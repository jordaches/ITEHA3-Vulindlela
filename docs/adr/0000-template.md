## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision? Refer to the Vulindlela scenario
or to your own code. Two or three paragraphs is plenty.

## Decision

What did you decide? Write it as a decision, not as a description. Include a
short code excerpt only if it makes the decision clearer.

## Alternatives considered

**<The alternative>.** Why you rejected it. Be specific. "It was worse" is not a
reason; "it only works inside one process, and the target architecture runs
several instances" is a reason.

List at least two. A decision with no rejected alternatives was not a decision.

## Consequences

**What we gain.** The benefit, stated plainly.

**What we accept.** The cost. Every real decision has one. An ADR with no cost
in it has not been thought through, and a marker will notice.

## Evidence (optional)

A test, a log extract or a screenshot showing the decision works.
